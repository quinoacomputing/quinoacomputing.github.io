Compute the multispecies viscous flux at an interior face.

Parameters
mat_blk in Material EOS block
ncomp in Number of components in this system
state in Left and right high-order face states
cellAvgState in Left and right cell-average states
fn in Face normal
centroid in Left and right element centroids
grad in Velocity and temperature gradients in left and right elements
fl in/out Numerical viscous flux using the Modified Gradient approach

The average gradient is modified according to Weiss et al. to obtain a stable discretization (average results in unstable central central difference). Ref: Weiss, J. M., Maruszewski, J. P., & Smith, W. A. (1999). Implicit solution of preconditioned Navier-Stokes equations using algebraic multigrid. AIAA journal, 37(1), 29-36.
